# NEGATIVE RESULT — the Sun-hora wealth direction is refuted
2026-07-23

## What was claimed, and by whom

I proposed it. From a pilot of three billionaires with usable birth times —
Gates 7, Musk 6, Ambani 5 grahas in the Sun's hora, mean 6.00 against a cohort
mean of 4.27 (n=93), permutation p=0.038 — I suggested the wealthy sit HIGH in
the Sun's hora: wealth that must be generated forges the large fortunes. I
pre-registered that direction one-tailed in hora_rules_prespecified.py, with the
three pilot charts excluded by name so they could not test their own hypothesis.

The product owner's PRIOR intuition was the opposite: the Moon's hora is wealth
that arrives easily, and he expected the wealthy to sit there.

## The confirmatory charts

Eight, all assigned group A (real wealth built) from the owner's descriptions
BEFORE scoring. Birth-time reliability tag in brackets.

    Akash          7   [B]
    Vijay Mallya   5   [A]  built then lost - see group note
    Channa         5   [A]
    Saket Burman   5   [A]  inherited + built a multi-billion empire
    Lalit Modi     3   [A]  built then lost
    Patricia       3   [B]
    Jaipuria       2   [A]
    Jessica        1   [A]
    ---------------------------------
    group A mean = 3.88          cohort mean = 4.27 (n=93)

## Result

    my pilot direction (wealthy HIGHER in the Sun's hora):   p = 0.815   REFUTED
    owner's direction  (wealthy LOWER, more Moon's hora):    p = 0.262   surviving, weak

Five of the eight wealthy charts sit AT or BELOW the cohort mean. The direction
I proposed is not merely unconfirmed, it is reversed: the wealthy in this cohort
lean, if anything, toward the Moon's hora — the way the owner said from the
start — though at p=0.26 that is not a claim either, just the surviving lean.

n=8 is still below the pre-registered minimum of 12, so neither direction gets a
verdict. But eight independent charts moving opposite to the pilot is enough to
stop calling the Sun-hora idea promising. It was three self-selected charts at
p=0.038, and it did exactly what an underpowered lucky finding does on new data.

## Why the pilot fooled me, written down so it fools me less next time

  - n=3, and I chose the three names already knowing they were billionaires.
  - p=0.038 is one lucky coin in a drawer full of coins. This repository has
    tested dozens of astrological rules; at p<0.05 roughly one in twenty of
    PURE NOISE clears the bar. 0.038 is not evidence, it is the base rate of
    self-deception.
  - The direction flattered a clean story ("large fortunes are forged"). Stories
    that read well are the ones to distrust most, which is the same lesson as
    the withdrawn Arudha fame rule and the withdrawn separation-timing claim.

## What this does NOT kill

H1 is the whole-chart split. H2-H6 (the house lords, the lagna, the Sri Lagna
lord in D-2) are separate rules and are individually underpowered here; they are
not adjudicated by this file. The DESCRIPTIVE reading in antar_engine/hora_chart.py
stands — it says what KIND of channel a chart has and never predicts an amount,
so nothing in it depended on the Sun-hora direction being true.

The built-versus-held question also stays open. Mallya and Modi (both "built
then lost") score 5 and 3; Saket Burman (built and holds) scores 5; there is no
visible separation, but n is far too small to see one if it exists.

## Consequence

The Sun-hora count must NOT be presented anywhere in the product as a wealth
signal, in either direction. It describes forged-versus-received as a matter of
character, and there it stays. Recorded alongside the other twenty-three dead
hypotheses in this directory.
