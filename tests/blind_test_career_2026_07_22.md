# Blind test — career mode, 2026-07-22

The first uncontaminated measurement of `antar_engine/career_mode.py`.

By this point the model had been rebuilt four times against the same ten charts
(3/6 → 3/6 → 7/10 → 8/10). Every step had a defensible reason, but all ten
outcomes were known, so improvement could no longer be distinguished from
fitting. These two charts were supplied with birth data ONLY. The predictions
below were written and sent before any outcome was disclosed.

## Outcomes

**Maneesh (Varanasi)** — trading business. Coal trading one day, converting
black money to white the next. A hustler. Previously employed.

  * nature TRADE — HIT, and literal rather than a stretched synonym.
  * ownership OWNED — HIT.
  * Unpredicted but consistent: no 8th-house involvement (venture was knocked
    down x0.4), which is what "nothing consistent, deal to deal" looks like —
    no outside capital, no durable structure.

**Sanjay Patel** — owns a restaurant. Previously employed.

  * ownership OWNED — HIT.
  * nature SERVICE — PARTIAL. A restaurant is service work, so the label
    defends itself, but the reading named "contracting, staffing, an agency, a
    trade" and said "people served rather than goods traded". Food was not
    mentioned. Scoring the label alone would be marking my own homework.
  * prominence −0.7 — correct. It was flagged in advance as the likely failure;
    a single restaurant is modest scale. The model was right and was mistrusted.

## Score

    ownership   2/2
    nature      1 hit, 1 partial
    total       3 to 3.5 of 4, blind

## What this does and does not establish

Encouraging, not established. Ownership is binary, so 2/2 occurs by chance one
time in four. Six to eight blind charts are needed before this is a finding.

The value of this file is the date on it: it is the record that the predictions
preceded the outcomes. Any future accuracy claim for this module should point
here, or be treated as fitted.
