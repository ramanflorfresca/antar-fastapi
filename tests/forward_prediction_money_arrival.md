Written 2026-07-23, BEFORE any of the predicted dates. That is the whole point
of this file: everything else in this directory was measured after the fact.

## Why the four earlier money events do NOT match, and why that helps

    13 Jul  LOSS casino        Moon in the 6th
    15 Jul  LOSS               Moon in the 7th
    16 Jul  GAIN speculation   Moon in the 8th
    18 Jul  LOSS speculation   Moon in the 8th
    23 Jul  FUNDS ARRIVED      Moon in the 11th, conjunct the 11th lord

Those four were SELF-INITIATED — a casino, a position taken. That is the 5th
house, risk a person chooses. This one was money coming FROM another party,
which is the 8th into the 11th. Different houses, different events. A rule that
separated them by accident would be suspicious; this one separates them along
the classical boundary.

## Base rate

    Moon in the 2nd or 11th                18% of days
    Moon conjunct the 11th lord (6 deg)      4% of days

## THE PREDICTION

Money arriving from another party — investor funds, a client payment, a
settlement, a repayment — clusters in these windows:

    2026-08-20
    2026-09-16
    2026-10-13
    2026-11-10
    2026-12-07
    2027-01-03
    2027-01-30

Self-initiated speculation should NOT cluster in them.

## How this is scored

Log every arrival of money from another party, with its date, whether or not it
falls in a window. Seven windows across six months is roughly 4% of days. If
received-money events land in them at anything close to chance, the rule is dead
and this file says so.

## The honest caveat

This was found AFTER the event was known, on n=1. A single post-hoc match at a
4% base rate is a hypothesis, not a finding — the fame rule looked at least as
good before its permutation test returned p = 0.152.

What makes it worth a forward test rather than the bin: the base rate was
measured before the claim, the mechanism is classical rather than invented, and
it makes a falsifiable statement about dates that have not happened yet.
