# Timing, not strength — the only test where nothing inverted

2026-07-23. Rules pre-specified in `85b0263` before scoring.

## The hypothesis

Seventeen natal-STRENGTH hypotheses had failed, with the direction consistently
backwards. This tests a different mechanism: not how strong a chart is, but WHEN
its wealth-giving periods fall.

Two people can carry identical wealth promise and live completely different
lives, because one receives his wealth mahadasha at 25 and the other at 70. Same
yogas, different birth minute, different dasha start. Nearly every chart holds
some wealth-giver; what differs is whether its period lands inside the years a
person can actually build.

## Result — 8 who built or earned substantially vs 19 ordinary

    rule                                     B       C      gap       p
    T1 productive wealth-years (25-65)   17.66   16.59    +1.06   0.386
    T2 longest single window             11.53   11.30    +0.23   0.456
    T3 age first window opens (earlier)  32.17   34.99    +2.82   0.285
    T4 fraction of 25-65 in a period      0.44    0.41    +0.03   0.386

Nothing survives the Bonferroni threshold of 0.0125.

## What is different, and what it is not

ALL FOUR POINT THE RIGHT WAY. The achievers hold more wealth-years, longer
single windows, a larger fraction of their productive span, and their first
window opens about three years earlier. In every strength test run here, four or
five of the rules INVERTED — the achievers scoring lower than the ordinary. Here
none does.

That is worth recording and it is NOT evidence. Four rules computed from the
same underlying quantity are not four independent tests, so "all four agree" is
close to one observation, not four. The effect sizes are also tiny: one extra
wealth-year out of forty. At n=8 an effect that small was never detectable, so
this is UNINFORMATIVE rather than negative — it neither supports the hypothesis
nor rules it out.

Testing it properly needs perhaps 40 in each group. That is a different order of
data collection than one product owner's contact list.

## What this settles for the product

The question "do I have a billionaire combination" asks for a magnitude. Across
twenty-one hypotheses — ten for fame, seven for success, four for timing — this
engine cannot produce one. That is now a measured statement rather than modesty.

What the chart CAN answer, and what should be said instead:

    WHICH KIND   service, trade or venture; owned or employed
    THROUGH WHAT the channel — the Sri Lagna's nakshatra lord
    FROM WHERE   the Sri Lagna's house — foreign, home, partners, public standing
    WHEN         the dasha windows, with dates, and how many productive
                 years remain inside them

For the chart this was built on: his longest wealth window opens 13 August 2026
and runs fourteen productive years — the last large one before his remaining
wealth-givers arrive past working age. That is a claim with a date on it, and it
can be wrong.

The number of zeros is not in the chart. Saying so is the difference between a
reading and a fortune.
