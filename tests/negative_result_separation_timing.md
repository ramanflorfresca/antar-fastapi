# Negative result — dasha does NOT time separation

2026-07-22. Recorded so it is not re-derived and re-believed.

## The hypothesis

The career finding held up well: Shashi's three dated transitions each landed on
the classically correct antardasha lord (7th lord for the partnership he lost,
Rahu for the disruptive venture, 6th lord for the service business). So the
obvious next step was whether the antardasha times SEPARATION the same way.

Predicted before computing: at a divorce, expect Saturn (karaka of separation)
or the 6th / 8th / 12th lord running at antardasha or pratyantardasha level.

## The data

    Raman     divorced 2014
    Kulbir    divorced 2009-2010
    Rishipal  divorced 2006-2007
    Shashi    marital problems, never separated  (the control)

## What it looked like at first

Every divorce window contained the pattern. Kulbir had Saturn at PD in Sep 2009
and Saturn at AD with the 6th lord beneath it in Sep 2010. Rishipal's whole
divorce period ran under Venus mahadasha — his 6th lord, the lord of conflict,
ruling the decade — with Saturn at PD in Sep 2006. Raman's 2013 showed the 12th
lord over the 6th lord, and 2014 showed Saturn at both AD and PD.

Three for three. It read beautifully.

## Why it is worthless

The base rate was never measured. When it was:

    Raman 84%   Kulbir 68%   Rishipal 69%   Shashi 87%

The "separation pattern" is present in 68-87% of ALL quarters across these
people's adult lives. Finding it at three divorce dates is exactly what finding
it at three dates chosen at random would look like.

Tightening the rule until it is rare enough to carry information destroys the
match:

    rule              base rate     hits at 7 divorce dates
    loose             68-87%        5/7
    both AD and PD    20-36%        2/7
    7th lord + both    0-9%         1/7
    Saturn at AD       8-13%        2/7

As the rule becomes discriminating, it stops matching. That is the definition of
no signal.

## What this does not overturn

The friction reading in `relationship.py` stands on its own evidence: Shashi
scores 3.7 (HIGH) and does have ongoing marital problems without separation,
which is precisely what a standing-pressure reading should produce. Friction is
a state, and it reads.

The career timing finding also stands, and the contrast is instructive. There
the antardasha lord was SPECIFIC to each event and different each time — 7th
lord, then Rahu, then 6th lord, matching three different kinds of transition. It
was not one pattern found in every window; it was three different patterns each
matching its own event. That is why it survives and this does not.

## What would be needed

A separation predictor cannot come from the dasha alone at this resolution.
Candidates not yet tested: transit Saturn crossing the natal 7th or its lord
(slow, rare, dateable), the varshphal for the year, or Saturn's return. All
require the base rate to be measured FIRST.

## The rule this establishes

Never report a timing pattern without its base rate. A pattern present most of
the time explains nothing, however well it fits the cases in front of you.
