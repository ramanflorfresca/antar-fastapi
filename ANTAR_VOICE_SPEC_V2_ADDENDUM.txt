1. TWO VOICE MODES
================================================================================
MODE: COACH VOICE (default for new users)
─────────────────────────────────────────
No planet names. Pure energy language. The user feels the reading
without learning any new vocabulary.

  "Your discipline energy is working quietly behind the scenes,
   in your daily routines and work life."

  "Three powerful energies are clustering in the part of your
   life connected to gains, friendships, and your professional
   network."

  "The first stretch is pure ignition. Think of it as lighting
   the fuse."

MODE: MENTOR VOICE (for returning/engaged users)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Planet names present with parenthetical translation. User builds
Vedic vocabulary through repetition.

  "Saturn planet of (discipline, structure) is supporting this
   from your daily work area."

  "Three planets gathered in your gains and networks area:
   Sun planet of (identity), Venus planet of (partnership),
   and Rahu planet of (ambition)."

  "The first sub-chapter is pure Rahu energy through April 2029
   — the ignition window."

KEY DIFFERENCE:
  Coach: "your discipline energy" — sounds like a part of YOU
  Mentor: "Saturn planet of (discipline)" — sounds like an external force

Coach Voice gives agency. Mentor Voice gives education.
Start with agency. Graduate to education when trust is built.

2. VOICE GRADUATION LOGIC
================================================================================
Track user engagement to determine voice mode:
  sessions_count: number of Ask Antar conversations
  days_active: days since first chart creation where user opened app
  practices_completed: total practice sessions marked complete

RULES:
  Coach Voice (default):
    sessions_count < 10 OR days_active < 14

  Mentor Voice (graduated):
    sessions_count >= 10 AND days_active >= 14

  Override: User can toggle in profile settings
    "Show me the planet names" → forces Mentor Voice
    "Keep it simple" → forces Coach Voice

IMPLEMENTATION:
  Add to charts table:
    voice_mode VARCHAR DEFAULT 'coach'  -- 'coach' | 'mentor' | 'auto'

  If voice_mode = 'auto' (default):
    Apply graduation rules above

  If voice_mode = 'coach' or 'mentor':
    User has explicitly chosen — respect it

4. PER-SURFACE VOICE MODE
================================================================================
ALWAYS COACH VOICE:
  - Onboarding (signature verification screen)
  - Welcome signal
  - Landing page / marketing
  - Share cards (social sharing)
  - Push notifications

VOICE MODE FOLLOWS GRADUATION:
  - Ask Antar (conversational)
  - Coming Up card (dashboard)
  - Daily Signal (Today tab)
  - Weekly briefing
  - Compatibility reading

ALWAYS MENTOR VOICE:
  - None. Even expert users benefit from Coach Voice on first
    encounter with a new feature.
